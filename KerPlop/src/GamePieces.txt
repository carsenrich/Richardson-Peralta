KerPlop Game Pieces Descriptions and Requirements
Authors: Carmela Richardson and Frank Peralta
Date: 9/23/26

We used a dinosaur theme for our Kerplot game using eight different 
pieces and two levels with different starting positions and setups.
All eight pieces are used in both levels.

DRAWABLE-ONLY PIECE (1)-

Mountain (M):
- implements Drawable directly
- stationery, does not interact with player

MOVING PIECES (2)-

TRex (T):
- extends GamePiece
- implements Moveable
- moves one space to the 
- stops when blocked or at left end of board
- updates board and stored location
- returns KILL when a player is within two spaces from trex
- no interaction otherwise

Raptor (R):
- extends GamePiece
- implements Moveable
- randomly moves one space to the left or 
- updates board and stored location
- returns HIT when player in on same or adjacent space
- no interaction otherwise

STATIONARY AND INTERACTIVE PIECES(5)-

Triceratops (C):
- extends GamePiece
- returns HIT when player is on same or adjacent space
- no interaction otherwise

Stegosaurus (S):
- extends GamePiece
- returns HIT when player is on same space
- no interaction otherwise

Pterodactyl (D):
- extends GamePiece
- returns ADVANCE when player is on same or adjacent space
- no interaction otherwise

Brontosaurus (B):
- extends GamePiece
- returns ADVANCE when player is on same space
- none otherwise

Prize ($):
- extends GamePiece
- returns GET_POINT when player lands on same space
- no interaction otherwise
- remains available and can reward more points when revisited
